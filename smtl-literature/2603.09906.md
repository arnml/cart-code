# Thinking to Recall

Identifier: arXiv:2603.09906  
Paper: *Thinking to Recall: How Reasoning Unlocks Parametric Knowledge in LLMs*  
Authors: Zorik Gekhman et al.  
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09906  
Related: [[search_more_think_less]]

## Short summary

This paper studies simple single-hop factual questions and finds that explicit reasoning can expand a model's parametric recall boundary. It is a useful contrast to SMTL because it shows extra reasoning can sometimes recover facts without external search, largely through computational buffering and factual priming, while also increasing hallucination risk when intermediate facts are wrong.
