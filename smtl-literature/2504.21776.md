# WebThinker

Identifier: arXiv:2504.21776  
Paper: *WebThinker: Empowering Large Reasoning Models with Deep Research Capability*  
Authors: Xiaoxi Li et al.  
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.21776  
Related: [[search_more_think_less]]

## Short summary

WebThinker lets the model interleave reasoning, web search, navigation, and report drafting. It supports SMTL's starting point because richer multi-step tool use improves deep research and complex reasoning benchmarks.
