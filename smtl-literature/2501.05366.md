# Search-o1

Identifier: arXiv:2501.05366  
Paper: *Search-o1: Agentic Search-Enhanced Large Reasoning Models*  
Authors: Xiaoxi Li et al.  
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.05366  
Related: [[search_more_think_less]]

## Short summary

Search-o1 augments long reasoning models with dynamic retrieval during inference. It supports the claim that adding search actions inside extended reasoning can improve performance on knowledge-intensive tasks.
