# Scaling Test-time Compute for LLM Agents

Identifier: arXiv:2506.12928  
Paper: *Scaling Test-time Compute for LLM Agents*  
Authors: King Zhu et al.  
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.12928  
Related: [[search_more_think_less]]

## Short summary

This paper studies agent scaling directly and reports that more test-time compute improves agent performance. Its strongest overlap with SMTL is the finding that diversified rollouts and added compute can help, even though SMTL argues that this scaling is often inefficient.
